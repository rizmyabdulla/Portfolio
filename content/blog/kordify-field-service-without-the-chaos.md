Field service work is messy in a very specific way. The office is juggling jobs, quotes and invoices. The technician is in a basement, on a rural road, or somewhere with no signal at all. Somewhere in between, a job gets lost, a photo never gets uploaded, or an invoice goes out a week late.

[Kordify](https://getkordify.com) is my attempt to tidy that up. It's built around one idea: **run a job from request to invoice in one place.**

## What it is

Kordify is a field service management platform for small teams. It has two halves:

- a **web app for the office**, where owners, admins and dispatchers create jobs, assign people and keep an eye on the day
- an **offline-capable mobile app for technicians**, so the work carries on even when the signal doesn't

It's in early access right now, and free to try.

## The job, from start to finish

Here is the flow I wanted to make feel simple.

1. **Set up your company.** Create a workspace in a couple of minutes, import your customers from a spreadsheet, and invite dispatchers and technicians by email.
2. **Dispatch and do the work.** Assign jobs from the web app. Technicians see them on their phone, work through the checklist, and capture photos and signatures, even offline.
3. **Close and invoice.** A job only completes once its requirements are met. Then it becomes an invoice, and reminders chase it until it's paid.

## Built for dead zones

The part I care about most is the technician app. It's designed for basements, rural roads and dead zones, so the things technicians do all day keep working with no connection:

- update a job's status and tick off checklist steps
- take before and after photos, stamped with time and place
- collect a customer's signature on screen

When the phone is back online, everything syncs in order. Evidence is time-stamped and can't be edited afterwards, which protects both the business and the customer.

## What's in the box today

Beyond the core flow, Kordify already covers a lot of day-to-day ground:

- a **dispatch board** with drive times, and a live **technician map** that warns you when a job is about to miss its deadline
- **checklists and proof of work** that can be made mandatory, so a job can't close until they're done
- **quotes** that customers accept or decline online, with an optional deposit
- **invoices, payments and credit notes**, with reminders for overdue ones
- **customer notifications**: booked, on the way, done, and a link to follow the job on a map
- **equipment and service history**, with QR labels technicians can scan on site
- **service agreements** for repeating visits
- **reports** on revenue, job profitability and technician time

There's more, including roles, an activity log, automation rules and optional AI help for writing quote notes and job summaries.
